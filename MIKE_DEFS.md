MIKE DEFINITIONS

To aid robot understanding.

- "VOS" aka "virtual-object-space" : The constellation of all virtual objects defined within this app, e.g. JS classes, css attributes, the fields of database tables. Refers more to non-primatives, e.g. a DinnerReservation class. Could be understood to include files and in some cases primatives.
- "MCS" aka "metaphysical-concept-space" : the total "real" territory of concepts which our custom VOS seeks to map to. For Amazon circa 1996, the MCS would be all books ever written, or at least published, their authors, as well as delivery orders, as well as customers. An Amazon customer, existing or potential, is part of the MCS, whereas the CUSTOMER_INFO sql table is part of the VOS.
